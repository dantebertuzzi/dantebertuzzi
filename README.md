### Dante Bertuzzi

Statistician and independent researcher in Petrolina, Brazil.
I write Julia packages that make Brazilian public data — health, elections,
crime, the central bank — reproducible to work with, plus a few tools I wanted
to exist.

**🇧🇷 Brazilian public data**

- [MicroSUS.jl](https://github.com/dantebertuzzi/MicroSUS.jl) — DATASUS microdata (SIM, SINASC, SIH, SIA, CNES, SINAN) with constant-memory streaming reads
- [BRElections.jl](https://github.com/dantebertuzzi/BRElections.jl) — TSE electoral data: candidates, votes by polling station, assets, electorate
- [ElectionForensics.jl](https://github.com/dantebertuzzi/ElectionForensics.jl) — election forensics tests, calibrated on your own data before you conclude anything
- [DeBRief.jl](https://github.com/dantebertuzzi/DeBRief.jl) — crime statistics from MJSP/Sinesp under one stable schema
- [BCB.jl](https://github.com/dantebertuzzi/BCB.jl) — Central Bank of Brazil APIs: SGS, PTAX, Focus and OData

**🛠️ Tools**

- [Perth.jl](https://github.com/dantebertuzzi/Perth.jl) — Gantt + Kanban project management, driven from the REPL or the browser
- [MissingPatterns.jl](https://github.com/dantebertuzzi/MissingPatterns.jl) — missing-data patterns in the terminal, no plotting dependencies
- [Kanon.jl](https://github.com/dantebertuzzi/Kanon.jl) — a document template language whose templates declare their own data contract
- [Liquid.jl](https://github.com/dantebertuzzi/Liquid.jl) — the Liquid template language in pure Julia, safe for untrusted templates

**🌊 Science**

- [OBISClient.jl](https://github.com/dantebertuzzi/OBISClient.jl) — Julia client for the Ocean Biodiversity Information System
- [meteo](https://github.com/dantebertuzzi/meteo) — less rain, more heat in Petrolina (1961–2025): a reproducible climate paper
